fxnkycat bass - VST3 bass chain plugin
Windows, 64-bit

INSTALL
1. Close your DAW.
2. Copy the "fxnkycat bass.vst3" folder from this zip into:
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
   (Windows may ask for admin permission - click Continue.)
3. Open your DAW and rescan plugins if it doesn't find it automatically.
4. Look for "fxnkycat bass" by "catfxnk creative" in your effects list.

Copy the whole "fxnkycat bass.vst3" folder, not just the file inside it.

WHAT'S INSIDE
Input HPF > Volume Rider > Comp-Gate > Problem EQ > De-Esser >
Compressor > Pultec EQ (order switchable) > Tape > Delay > Filters,
with a before/after spectrum analyzer and activity lights on every stage.
Every stage starts OFF - switch on the ones you want.

Tempo follows your project automatically.
